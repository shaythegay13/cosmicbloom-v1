# 🎮 Character Abilities - Now Active!

All character abilities are now **fully implemented** in the game!

## ☀️ Sunny - The Optimistic Farmer
**Ability**: Crops grow 10% faster

**How it works:**
- All crops planted by Sunny grow 10% faster than normal
- 30-second growth stage becomes 27 seconds
- Great for players who want quick harvests

**Best for**: Fast-paced farming, quick coin generation

---

## 🌙 Luna - The Night Gardener
**Ability**: Night crops yield double

**How it works:**
- Harvesting crops between **6 PM and 6 AM** gives **2x coins**
- A 15-coin carrot becomes 30 coins at night!
- Perfect for night owl players

**Best for**: Players who play in the evening/night, maximum profit

---

## 🌍 Terra - The Earth Whisperer
**Ability**: Soil quality bonus 15%

**How it works:**
- All harvested crops sell for **15% more coins**
- A 15-coin carrot sells for 17 coins
- Consistent bonus regardless of time

**Best for**: Steady income, reliable farming

---

## 💧 River - The Water Keeper
**Ability**: Water efficiency 20%

**How it works:**
- **20% chance** to automatically water adjacent crops when watering
- Can water up to 4 extra crops at once (up, down, left, right)
- Saves time and effort

**Best for**: Large farms, efficiency, less clicking

---

## 🧙 Sage - The Wise Cultivator
**Ability**: Experience gain 25%

**How it works:**
- Earn **25% more XP** from all farming activities
- 5 XP from harvest becomes 6 XP
- Level up faster than other characters

**Best for**: Players who want to unlock features quickly, completionists

## 🎯 Strategy Tips

### For Maximum Coins:
1. **Luna** (if you play at night) - 2x yield is huge!
2. **Terra** - Consistent 15% bonus
3. **Sunny** - More harvests = more coins over time

### For Fast Progression:
1. **Sage** - 25% XP boost
2. **Sunny** - Faster crops = more actions = more XP

### For Convenience:
1. **River** - Less watering needed
2. **Sunny** - Less waiting for crops

## 🧪 Testing Your Ability

### Test Sunny:
1. Plant a crop (normally 30 seconds per stage)
2. Water it
3. It should grow in ~27 seconds

### Test Luna:
1. Wait until after 6 PM (18:00)
2. Harvest a crop
3. Check console - you'll see "🌙 Luna night bonus: Double yield!"
4. You'll get 2x coins

### Test Terra:
1. Harvest any crop
2. A 15-coin carrot gives 17 coins
3. A 20-coin tomato gives 23 coins

### Test River:
1. Plant multiple crops next to each other
2. Water one crop
3. ~20% chance adjacent crops also get watered
4. Check console for "River bonus: Also watered crop at..."

### Test Sage:
1. Do any farming activity
2. Check XP gained
3. Harvesting gives 6 XP instead of 5
