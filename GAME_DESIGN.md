# Farm & Give - Game Design Document

## 🎮 Core Concept

**Farm & Give** is a casual farming simulation game where players grow crops, manage their farm, and contribute to real-world community gardens. The unique selling point is that 10% of all in-game purchases directly support local urban farms and community gardens.

## 🎯 Target Audience

### Primary
- **Age**: 18-35
- **Interests**: Casual gaming, sustainability, social impact
- **Gaming Experience**: Casual to moderate
- **Values**: Community, environment, giving back

### Secondary
- Families looking for wholesome games
- Educators teaching about agriculture
- Community garden supporters

## 🌱 Crops System

### Crop Categories

#### Vegetables
- **Carrot** 🥕 - 30s/stage, 4 stages, sells for 15 coins
- **Tomato** 🍅 - 40s/stage, 4 stages, sells for 20 coins
- **Corn** 🌽 - 35s/stage, 4 stages, sells for 18 coins
- **Potato** 🥔 - 25s/stage, 3 stages, sells for 12 coins

#### Grains
- **Wheat** 🌾 - 25s/stage, 3 stages, sells for 12 coins
- **Rice** 🍚 - 30s/stage, 3 stages, sells for 14 coins

#### Fruits (Planned)
- **Strawberry** 🍓 - 45s/stage, 4 stages, sells for 25 coins
- **Watermelon** 🍉 - 60s/stage, 5 stages, sells for 40 coins

### Growth Mechanics
1. **Plant** - Place seed in tilled soil
2. **Water** - Crop needs water to progress
3. **Grow** - Automatic progression through stages
4. **Harvest** - Collect when fully grown

### Advanced Mechanics (Planned)
- **Seasons** - Crops grow better in certain seasons
- **Quality** - Better care = higher quality = more coins
- **Cross-breeding** - Combine crops for new varieties
- **Fertilizer** - Speed up growth or increase yield

## 🏆 Progression System

### Experience & Levels
- **Level 1-10**: Beginner farmer (100 XP per level)
- **Level 11-25**: Experienced farmer (200 XP per level)
- **Level 26-50**: Master farmer (500 XP per level)
- **Level 51+**: Legendary farmer (1000 XP per level)

### XP Sources
- Tilling soil: 1 XP
- Planting seed: 2 XP
- Watering crop: 1 XP
- Harvesting crop: 5 XP
- Selling produce: 1 XP per 10 coins
- Completing quests: 50-500 XP

### Unlocks by Level
- **Level 5**: Unlock new area
- **Level 10**: Unlock animals
- **Level 15**: Unlock greenhouse
- **Level 20**: Unlock advanced crops
- **Level 25**: Unlock crafting
- **Level 30**: Unlock trading

## 💰 Economy

### Currency
- **Coins** - Primary currency, earned by selling crops
- **Gems** (Planned) - Premium currency, earned or purchased

### Pricing Strategy
- **Seeds**: 5-20 coins
- **Tools**: 50-200 coins
- **Decorations**: 10-100 coins
- **Expansions**: 500-5000 coins
- **Premium Items**: 1-10 gems

### Monetization (Ethical)
- **Free to Play**: All core features free
- **Optional Purchases**: Cosmetics, convenience items
- **No Pay-to-Win**: Purchases don't give gameplay advantages
- **Charity First**: 10% of ALL revenue to community gardens

## 🌍 Charity Integration

### How It Works
1. Player makes in-game purchase
2. 10% automatically allocated to charity fund
3. Player selects their local community garden
4. Quarterly distributions to selected gardens
5. Players see their impact in real-time

### Charity Features
- **Search** - Find gardens by location
- **Profiles** - Learn about each garden
- **Impact** - See total donations and supporters
- **Updates** - Receive news from supported gardens
- **Verification** - All charities verified and legitimate

### Transparency
- Public donation totals
- Quarterly impact reports
- Photos and updates from gardens
- Financial transparency
