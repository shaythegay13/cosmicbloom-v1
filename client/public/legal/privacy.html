Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026 · applies to the Farm & Give mobile apps and website

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address, username and a securely hashed password, used solely to operate your account and sync your save.
  • Gameplay data: your save file (farms, coins, level, settings) stored on our servers so you can play across devices.
  • Purchase records: if you buy a coin pack, Apple/Google process the payment and our payment partner RevenueCat notifies us of the purchase (product, price, anonymized transaction ID). We never see your payment card details.

What we do NOT do

  • No advertising and no advertising SDKs.
  • No tracking across apps or websites; no sale of personal data.
  • No analytics profiles tied to your identity.
  • No collection of contacts, location, photos, or device identifiers beyond what is technically required to run the game.

Charity donations

We donate 10% of our proceeds to community gardens. Donations are made by us from our own revenue; your purchase is a purchase of virtual goods, not a charitable contribution by you, and is not tax-deductible.

Data retention & deletion

You can permanently delete your account at any time from Menu → Delete account inside the app. This erases your account and save data immediately; purchase records are anonymized (no longer linked to you) so our charity accounting stays accurate. You can also email us at privacy@farmandgive.example.

Children

Farm & Give is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Passwords are stored hashed with bcrypt. Save data is stored in access-controlled databases.

Contact

Questions? Email privacy@farmandgive.example.
